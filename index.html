How to Play

1
Inspect the Arrow Paths: Arrows are continuous lines with 90° right-angle turns ending with a directional arrowhead.
2
Tap to Escape: When you tap an arrow, it shoots forward in the direction of its arrowhead and follows its path out of the board.
3
Watch Out for Blockers: If another arrow crosses its escape path, it cannot leave. Tapping a blocked arrow costs you 1 heart (❤️).
4
Use Power-Ups: Stuck? Tap Hint to highlight a safe arrow. Made a mistake? Tap Undo to reverse your last move.
